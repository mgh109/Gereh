# Brand images

These assets identify the shoe brands sold by the store. Trademarks remain the property of their owners.

- Adidas, Fila, Nike, Puma, Reebok, Under Armour: Simple Icons (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons).
- Altra, Brooks, Hi-Tec, LOWA, Mizuno, Prince, RBX, Rocket Dog, Ryka, Salomon, Saucony, Skechers: logo assets from each brand's public official website.
- ASICS: https://cdn.worldvectorlogo.com/logos/asics-6.svg.
- New Balance, HOKA, The North Face, GEREH: existing approved project assets.
- Newfeel keeps its text label until a verified logo asset is supplied.

Files are served locally. The idempotent brand-logo migration and SQL update only fill empty `logo_path` fields; they preserve customized logos.
